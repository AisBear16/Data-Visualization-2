A data story about board games

Roll the Dice: The World of Board Games

From CATAN to Monopoly, board games bring friends and families around the same table. But who plays them around the world, which kinds are most popular, and are the hardest games really the best?

Part 1

Where the world's board gamers are

BoardGameGeek is the world's largest board game website, where players rate games and log their collections. More than 400,000 of its members say which country they live in, which gives us a map of the hobby.

Part 2

What kinds of games are out there?

BoardGameGeek sorts games into eight broad categories, such as family games, party games and strategy games. "Wargames" recreate battles, and "thematic" games tell a story.

Part 3

Is harder better?

Each game has a complexity score, also called its "weight". Players vote from 1 (simple rules) to 5 (many rules and long-term planning). The score is the average of those votes.

Key takeaway

Games with a complexity of 4 or more average 7.5 out of 10, while games under 2 average 6.0. Players who rate games on BoardGameGeek seem to reward depth.

Part 4

What makes a hit?

The best-rated games and the most-owned games are not the same. The top-ranked games are long and complex, while the games in the most homes are quick and easy to learn.
